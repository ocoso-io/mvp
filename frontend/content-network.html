How OCOSO uses blockchain for your protection

Your work becomes a NFT. It stays yours. It earns. It can’t be stolen.

That means your content is immutable, always linked to you, and AI-ready: machines can read it, quote it, and pay for its use. From the moment you mint, your rights are locked in and your work can’t be silently stolen or repurposed.
