II. Rights of Users and Data Subjects

With regard to the data processing described in more detail below, users and data subjects have the right to obtain confirmation as to whether personal data concerning them are being processed, access to the data processed, further information about the data processing, and copies of the data (see Art. 15 GDPR).

To receive the data concerning them which they have provided and to transmit those data to other controllers (see Art. 20 GDPR).

To lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority if they believe that the data concerning them are processed by the provider in breach of data protection law (see Art. 77 GDPR).

In addition, the provider is obliged to inform all recipients to whom data have been disclosed of any rectification or erasure of data or restriction of processing carried out pursuant to Articles 16, 17(1), or 18 GDPR. This obligation does not exist insofar as such notification proves impossible or involves a disproportionate effort. Users and data subjects also have the right, under Art. 21 GDPR, to object at any time to the future processing of data concerning them where the data are processed by the provider on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. In particular, an objection to processing for the purposes of direct marketing is permitted.

Data processed in connection with the use of our website will be deleted or blocked as soon as the purpose of storage ceases to apply, provided that no statutory retention obligations oppose deletion and unless otherwise stated for specific processing operations below.

 
Server Data

For technical reasons—particularly to ensure a secure and stable website—data are transmitted by your internet browser to us and/or to our web space provider. These so-called server log files include, inter alia, the type and version of your internet browser, the operating system, the website from which you arrived at our website (referrer URL), the web pages of our site that you visit, the date and time of each access, and the IP address of the internet connection from which our website is accessed.

The data collected in this way are stored temporarily, but not together with other personal data.

The legal basis for this storage is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in improving the stability, functionality and security of our website.

As a rule, the data are deleted after seven days at the latest unless further storage is required for evidentiary purposes. In that case, the data are wholly or partially excluded from deletion until the incident has been finally clarified.

 
Cookies

a) Session cookies
We use cookies on our website. Cookies are small text files or other storage technologies placed and stored on your device by your internet browser. Through these cookies, certain information is processed, such as your browser and location data and your IP address, to an individual extent.

Processing via cookies makes our website more user-friendly, effective and secure, e.g., by enabling the display of our website in different languages or a shopping basket function.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR insofar as cookies process data for the initiation or performance of a contract.

If processing does not serve contractual purposes, our legitimate interest lies in improving the functionality of our website. In that case, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser.

b) Third-party cookies
Our website may also use cookies from partner companies with whom we cooperate for advertising, analytics or functional purposes.

Details, in particular regarding the purposes and legal bases of such third-party cookie processing, can be found below in this privacy notice.

c) Options to remove cookies
You can prevent or restrict the installation of cookies by adjusting your browser settings. You can also delete stored cookies at any time. The steps and measures required for this depend on the specific browser you are using. If you have questions, please use the browser’s help function or documentation, or contact the manufacturer or support. Processing of so-called Flash cookies cannot be prevented via the browser settings. Instead, you must change your Flash player settings. The necessary steps and measures depend on the Flash player you use; please refer to the help or documentation of your Flash player or contact the manufacturer or user support.

If you prevent or restrict the installation of cookies, not all functions of our website may be fully usable.
