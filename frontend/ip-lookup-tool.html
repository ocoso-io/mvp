Know Your Rights. Protect Your Content.

Every country, every law – one tool to see where you stand.

 

The rules of AI scraping and text-and-data mining (TDM) are not the same everywhere. Some countries grant commercial users broad exceptions, others require explicit licenses, and many leave creators in legal grey zones. That means your protection — and your risk — depends on where you publish from.

OCOSO built this tool to cut through the complexity. Enter your country, and you’ll see in seconds: whether machine-readable opt-outs protect you, which specific limitations apply, what countermeasures we recommend, and the original sources of law and guidance.
